## Confirmed findings requiring correction

1. **[P2] Failed nested ownership check pops the outer local cooldown scope.** In reviewed `dungeon_result_stats.js:35`, `owner()` runs before `this.frame` is constructed/pushed. Its explicit owner-mismatch exception therefore leaves this invocation absent from the stack. The corresponding `onLeave` at line39 still unconditionally pops. If an outer local parent is active, the nested invocation pops that outer frame, then throws the scope error. After restoring the owner, a native successful outer delta cannot find its frame and is dropped. `independent-cd-review-v2.cjs/json` reproduces an acceleration outer scope, nested discrete ownership mismatch, recovery, and outer delta0.5: actual extension rows are empty; both nested and outer leaves report a scope error. Frida JS callback exceptions do not cancel the native call or its leave callback. Push a per-invocation unqualified frame before qualification; only this invocation may pop its frame.

2. **[P2] Individually accepted finite CD reports can break clear-result sealing.** `pvp_play_messages.hp_report(...,statistics=True)` accepts any finite nonnegative f32; the report handler accepts the new scoped map. The seal loop sums a provider across recipients in Python float, then `member_payload` packs the total as f32. Two admitted recipients can each report the largest finite f32 for one provider: all reports receive ACK0; `seal` raises `OverflowError: float too large to pack with f format`. This allows display-only telemetry to fail the post-clear sealing path. `independent-cd-sum-boundary-v2.py/json` uses a private three-member in-memory room and validates this exact accepted input; no databases. Establish f32-representable aggregation before accepting a new report, or otherwise ensure a bad telemetry total cannot enter/fail the committed result path; do not invent a gameplay cap or silently clamp the displayed total.
