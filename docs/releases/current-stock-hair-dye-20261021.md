# Dye admission for234 qualified owned hair templates

An unconditional server hold rejected234 permanent hair templates even though the current Chinese official opening table includes their eight families. This change removes exactly those234 IDs from the held list. The remaining354 holds, all palettes, dye parts, ticket ranges and consumption rules are unchanged. Players must already own a qualified item UID; the existing server validates ownership and eligibility, consumes the palette atomically and stores colors. Native preview, rendering and effects remain in the client.

Sources retrieved2026-10-21: the Chinese official [hair-dye opening table](https://els.ztgame.com/game/004-1682-00377-506737.shtml?ms=885&mt=887) and [wardrobe/dye guide](https://els.ztgame.com/game/004-1682-00376-497051.shtml?ms=885&mt=886). These are current CN guide pages with historical opening dates; they establish the listed operation, not a present acquisition channel for every item. The Elwiki pages were unavailable with403 and supply no additional rule.

This release therefore applies to the reviewed current stock factory closure. It does not implement a future positive-event guard: adding factories, event producers or merger inputs requires renewed qualification before using this admission policy.
